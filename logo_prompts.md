# Nano Banana Pro - AI Logo Prompt Templates

Use these templates to generate high-value, professional logos. Replace `[BUSINESS NAME]` and `[INDUSTRY]` with your client's details.

## 1. Minimalist Tech (SaaS, Startups)

**Prompt:**
> A minimalist geometric logo for a [INDUSTRY] brand named "[BUSINESS NAME]", utilizing negative space and clean lines, vector style, flat design, matte black and electric blue color palette, on a clean white background, Behance top feature, Paul Rand style.

## 2. Luxury & Heritage (Real Estate, Law, Finance)

**Prompt:**
> A premium luxury logo emblem for "[BUSINESS NAME]", [INDUSTRY], featuring an intertwined monogram with serif typography, gold foil texture effect on dark charcoal leather texture background, embossed elegant look, cinematic lighting, 8k resolution.

## 3. Cybernetic / Futurist (Gaming, AI, Crypto)

**Prompt:**
> A futuristic cyberpunk logo for "[BUSINESS NAME]", glowing neon cyan and magenta gradients, metallic chrome finish, 3D render style, glitch effect, tech-noir atmosphere, dark background, octane render.

## 4. Organic & Natural (Health, Wellness, Eco)

**Prompt:**
> A soft organic logo for "[BUSINESS NAME]", watercolor style, botanical illustration elements wrapping around the typography, sage green and earth tones, textured paper background, hand-drawn aesthetic.

## 5. Industrial & Bold (Construction, Logistics, Gym)

**Prompt:**
> A bold masculine logo for "[BUSINESS NAME]", heavy block typography, stencil style, brushed steel texture, orange safety accents, drop shadow, strong impact, on a concrete background.

## 6. Retro / Vintage (Coffee, Barber, Apparel)

**Prompt:**
> A vintage badge logo for "[BUSINESS NAME]", 1950s Americana style, distressed texture, muted retro colors (mustard, navy, cream), vector illustration, circular emblem format.

## 7. Abstract 3D (Creative Agencies, Marketing)

**Prompt:**
> An abstract 3D glassmorphism logo for "[BUSINESS NAME]", impossible geometry shape, frosted glass texture with subsurface scattering, soft pastel gradients, studio lighting, clay render style, C4D.

## 8. Corporate Trust (Consulting, Insurance)

**Prompt:**
> A trustworthy corporate logo for "[BUSINESS NAME]", abstract shield or pillar icon, reliable navy blue and grey color scheme, sans-serif modern typography, balanced symmetry, professional and established look.
